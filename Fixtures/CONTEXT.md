# `Fixtures/` context

The fixtures are small, controlled corpora for manually validating the preview
and link, image, and LaTeX contracts. They are not production data and must not
contain personal documents.

- [`MarkdownValidation/CONTEXT.md`](MarkdownValidation/CONTEXT.md) — local and remote images, and common mathematical notation.
- [`markdown-link-test/CONTEXT.md`](markdown-link-test/CONTEXT.md) — external links and supported or unsupported local files.
- [`latex-preview-test/CONTEXT.md`](latex-preview-test/CONTEXT.md) — a multipage LaTeX document, dependencies, references, and bibliography.

Historical validation results are kept in [`docs/reference/`](../docs/reference/CONTEXT.md); instructions for a specific fixture are kept alongside its files.
